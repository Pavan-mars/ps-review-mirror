Cell 20 stores `_res["predictions"] = _test_pdf` -- the held-out TEST SPLIT.
Cell 24 exports exactly that to chicago/device_ps1_cross_wired_daily/. So the
prefix with "daily" in its name holds one-off scores on training data. The
word describes the grain, not the cadence.

cross_wired_daily_job.py then filters `transit_day IN (target_days)` against
that prefix. On a new day it matches zero rows. Zero rows is not an exception,
so the try/except never fires, _missing_ps1_cats stays empty, and nothing
prints. The two guards (dupes>0, row_count==0) both pass because the spine is
full. The job succeeds and writes a cross-wire where every PS1 prediction is
NULL.

That is the fourth time this programme has met the same failure: a WHERE
clause that drops every row says nothing -- it does not say "no data". It is
why v_ps1_xw_causation needed sufficient_data, why the freshness verdict had
to make UNREADABLE dominate, and why sql/54 had to undo sql/53. Here it would
have reached the dashboard as a blank prediction on day one of Chicago data.

THE SERVING CONTRACT, read from cell 22 rather than assumed
  The archive at sagemaker/ps1-3d/<tag>/spark-model-v<n>/model.tar.gz is
  self-contained: a native booster file, ps1_<tag>_meta.joblib carrying
  feature_cols and medians, ps1_<tag>_threshold.joblib, and an inference.py
  generated inline from _INF_TEMPLATE. Managed DLC is sufficient; ECR is not
  required for PS1. docker/inference_ps1.py is a separate BYOC path that
  nothing deploys.

  A first pass of this design inferred the serving contract FROM
  docker/inference_ps1.py and got two answers wrong -- it reported the
  artefact as a pickled sklearn estimator, and rejected Batch Transform on
  the grounds that the handler carries no column names. Both are false of
  the deployed handler. The wrong reasoning is struck in place in the design
  document rather than deleted, because the error has a shape worth keeping
  visible: evidence that something COULD be true, treated as evidence that
  it WAS. Same shape as the partition_cols finding and as sql/53.

C-3, the highest-severity defect found in this review
  _predict_proba does .reindex(columns=feature_cols, fill_value=0.0), while
  _impute only fills NaN for columns already present. So a feature that is
  present-but-null gets its median, and a feature that is ABSENT gets 0.0 --
  and 0.0 is an ordinary value for a count or a rate. A Gold column that
  fails to materialise on a given day produces a confident, plausible, wrong
  probability with nothing recorded anywhere. The scoring job must assert
  column presence before serialising. DECISION-8.
